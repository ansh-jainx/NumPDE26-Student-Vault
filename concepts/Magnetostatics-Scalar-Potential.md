# Magnetostatics Scalar Potential

**Reference:** §13.3.3.1

---

## Potential formulation

For admissible source currents and topology assumptions, magnetostatic fields can be represented through a scalar potential (in form language and vector proxies).

This leads to an elliptic variational problem in a scalar space, similar in spirit to electrostatic Poisson problems.

## Practical use

- Good baseline model for simply connected settings.
- Reuses scalar FEM intuition from early weeks.
- Serves as comparison against vector-potential and saddle-point formulations.
